The Stoo Story

It all starts with the "Peace" sign and a passion for broth. In the heart of Palawan, our recipe was born from a mix of traditions—the savory Filipino spirit and the aromatic Vietnamese soul.

Every bowl of Stoo Chaolong is a labor of love. We don't cut corners. We don't rush the steam. We just serve happiness, one reddish-gold bowl at a time.
